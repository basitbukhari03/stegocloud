How It Works

Two layers of security — invisible to the naked eye

Layer 1: AES-256

Your secret message is encrypted with AES-256-CBC using a password-derived key. Even if someone extracts the data, it's unreadable without the password.

Layer 2: Steganography

The encrypted data is hidden in the Least Significant Bits of image pixels. The image looks completely identical to the human eye.

MFA Authentication

Every account is protected by Time-based One-Time Passwords (TOTP) compatible with Google Authenticator. No data without two factors.
